TRACKING DREAMS

"Stories from the FORTY"

I started hunting when I was 12 years old and the change in the temperature and in the air signaled the start of a new season. It's not just about the harvest; its about the stillness before dawn, the meticulous planning, and the deep respect for the land and the game. My first hunt with my Dad in the crisp Minnesota air, and that connection to the wild has only deepened. This site is a record of those experiences with family.

The pursuit of the perfect buck is a lifelong endeavor, and every season teaches me something new. I believe in ethical hunting, conservation, and passing on the tradition.

— Scott Rindahl, Dedicated Outdoorsman
